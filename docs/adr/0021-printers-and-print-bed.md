## Context

For FDM printing (ADR-0002) it must be visible while designing whether a part fits the bed.
Beds differ per printer, and many have particularities: areas that must not be printed on
(Bambu X1/P1: front left corner 18 × 28 mm; Qidi Q1 Pro: cut-outs at the back), strips that
only one nozzle reaches (dual-nozzle printers such as the Bambu H2D), round beds (delta
printers). A first hand-maintained list of five rectangular beds went stale at once. The slicer
profiles of OrcaSlicer (fork of Bambu Studio and PrusaSlicer) contain exactly this geometry for
more than 400 models. With that many entries the list is long; someone who works with two or
three printers wants them at the top and possibly the rest hidden.

This topic absorbs the former decisions 0016 and 0023.

## Decision

### Generated database

- `crates/qoob3d-core/src/printer/data.rs` is generated by `cargo xtask printers <OrcaSlicer
  resources/profiles>`; the OrcaSlicer commit is in the file header. **Never edit it by hand.**
  It currently holds 441 models; the default is the Bambu Lab P1S.
- The generator reads `machine_model_list` per vendor, prefers the 0.4 mm variant and resolves
  `inherits`. Only facts are taken over: `printable_area` (polygon), `bed_exclude_area`
  (even-odd polygon), `extruder_printable_area` (per nozzle) and `printable_height`. Pseudo
  vendors (`Custom`, `OrcaArena`) stay out; profiles with more than 192 polygon points in total
  (`MAX_BED_POINTS`, the uniform array size) are skipped and reported.
- `PrinterProfile` (core) is static data. `bed()` returns a `BedGeometry` centred on the world
  origin: slicers arrange parts themselves, only size and shape matter for design.

### Print bed in the grid shader

- Grid, bed and X/Y axes are one analytic full-screen pass (`fs_grid`): intersection of the
  view ray with z = 0, lines smoothed with `fwidth`, grid spacing in powers of ten adapted to
  the zoom, fade towards the horizon and at grazing angles. It writes `frag_depth` and is depth
  tested against the bodies.
- Bed polygons go into a uniform array; per pixel an even-odd test (`poly_inside`, works for
  holes and self-intersecting zones) and the distance to the edge for pixel-wide outlines.
  Printable area light with a grey outline; **exclusion zones hatched amber**; **zones only
  one nozzle reaches tinted amber**; optionally the **build volume** as a wireframe up to the
  maximum height (`build_volume_segments`).
- `render-project` draws the default printer's bed.

### Picking a printer, "my printers"

- The printer picker in the navigation bar has a search field (name and vendor) and lists
  printers grouped by vendor; markers ▣ (exclusion zone) and ◑ (single-nozzle zones); the
  tooltip shows dimensions and notes.
- A star (☆ / ★) marks a printer as a favourite: `Settings::favorite_printers` in the order
  added, listed under "my printers" at the top. ⚙ Settings → Printers can hide all others
  (`Settings::only_favorite_printers`) and remove favourites; with no favourites all printers
  are shown, with a hint. Both are persisted (ADR-0020).
- The list closes only on a click outside (`CloseOnClickOutside`) or after a selection; on
  opening, the search is cleared and focused.

## Consequences

- New printer models arrive by regenerating from a newer OrcaSlicer version.
- OrcaSlicer is AGPL-3.0. Only dimensions and coordinates (facts) are taken over, no texts or
  images; this is part of the licence review before distributing binaries.
- The grid is infinite and needs no geometry buffer.
- A warning "part lies in an exclusion zone / exceeds the build volume" can later be a pure
  geometry check with the same polygons.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Hand-maintained list** – goes stale, special zones get forgotten.
- **Triangulating the polygons** – fails on the self-intersecting exclusion zones of some
  profiles (e.g. Qidi); the even-odd test is robust and exact.
- **Grid as line geometry** – finite, aliasing, rebuilt when zooming.
- **Bed origin in the corner like the printer coordinate system** – irrelevant for design and
  confusing.
- **Recently used printers automatically at the top** – unpredictable; the user wants a fixed
  selection. A separate dialog for managing printers is too heavy for a list with stars.
